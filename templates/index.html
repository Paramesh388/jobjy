How the Recommendation Engine Works

A structured, multi-stage NLP & Machine Learning pipeline

1. Resume Parsing

Securely extracts plain text from multi-page PDFs, Word documents, or plain text files using standard text decoders.

2. NLP Entity Extraction

Identifies candidate skills across technologies, detects education credentials (B.Tech, BCA, etc.), and estimates experience level.

3. TF-IDF & Cosine Math

Converts resume and live job descriptions into n-gram TF-IDF vectors, evaluating cosine distance for semantic relevance.

4. Ranking & Skill Gaps

Produces ranked recommendations showing matched vs missing skills, plus frequency analytics of in-demand market skills.

AIML Methodology

Transparent Dual-Score Matching Model

Unlike black-box models or randomized percentages, JobMatch AI uses an explainable dual-metric formula combining natural language vector semantics with direct skill overlap:

Mathematical Relevance Formula:
Final Relevance Score = (Text Similarity × 0.60) + (Skill Overlap Ratio × 0.40)
  • TF-IDF Vectorization: Weighs informative technical keywords using term frequency and inverse document frequency.
  • Cosine Similarity: Computes the cosine of the angle between two sparse vectors in high-dimensional feature space.
  • Skill Overlap Matrix: Calculates the exact proportion of employer-required skills satisfied by the candidate.
